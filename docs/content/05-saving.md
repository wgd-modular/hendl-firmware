# Saving & Recall

hendl remembers how you left it. For each channel it stores:

- the active **mode**, and
- the **looping / triggered** operating mode.

## Automatic saving

There is nothing to press. A couple of seconds after your last change, hendl
writes the current mode and operating mode of all three channels to its internal
memory. Waiting for a short pause means rapidly stepping through modes does not
wear out the memory — only where you finally settle is stored.

Knob positions and CV are live, physical signals and are never stored; only the
per-channel mode and operating-mode selections are remembered.

## On power-up

When you switch on, hendl restores the last saved modes and operating modes, so
your instrument comes back exactly as you left it. The very first time it runs —
or if the stored data is ever unreadable — every channel starts on **LFO**,
**looping**.
