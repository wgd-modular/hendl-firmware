# The Modes

Each channel holds four modes, selected by short-pressing the channel button.
The first three are the same on every channel. The fourth is different on each,
giving hendl its three signature generators.

| # | LEDs | Mode | On channel |
|---|------|------|------------|
| 1 | ○ ○ | **LFO** | all |
| 2 | ○ ● | **Envelope** | all |
| 3 | ● ○ | **Sample & Hold** | all |
| 4 | ● ● | **Unique** | 1 Turing · 2 Euclidean · 3 Smooth Random |

Every mode responds to the **looping / triggered** long-press, and every mode
has a single **CV destination** that comes alive when you patch its input. The
chapters that follow give, for each mode:

- what **Pot 1** and **Pot 2** do,
- what the **CV input** modulates,
- what the **trigger** does, and
- how **looping** and **triggered** differ.

Read the three shared modes first — they behave identically on all three
channels — then the three unique modes.
